## Problem Statement

The Workspace Owner cannot yet rely on the combined local changes for setup, Person Research,
Meeting Debriefs, and content workflows. Approved fixes exist across seven local commits and an
interrupted working-tree implementation, but the combined result has not passed the delivery gates
or reached `main`. The branch name describes only one small part of the work.

The September 24, 2026 audit found two distinct blockers. The documented Guided Setup invocation
exits before its first stage because its default installation environment path fails its own
absolute-path preflight. Separately, Person Research contracts regress in the committed local
changes: representative operation-history, dossier-query, and source-accounting tests pass on
remote `main` and fail on local committed HEAD. Removing the uncommitted setup work would leave
those failures in place.

The Installation Operator needs a complete setup and migration procedure that preserves existing
credentials and Workspace records. The Workspace Owner needs truthful setup guidance and research
results whose evidence, history, and lifecycle remain coherent. The maintainer needs the work
completed, verified, reviewed, and delivered through traceable PRs without losing unrelated local
research or UX evidence.

## Solution

Complete the existing approved issue queue as one coordinated integration effort. Preserve the
working tree, repair Person Research regressions at the existing product interfaces, finish
installation-owned credential provisioning and explicit migration, and retain the meeting-specific
onboarding improvements. Verify the smaller committed UX and Debrief fixes against their existing
issue briefs instead of treating their commit messages as completion evidence.

Deliver independently reviewable changes through the repository's required PR workflow. Reconcile
the overlapping Brand Profile scan PR before delivering duplicate work. Keep the Jev research and
UX reports separate from application implementation. Existing issue briefs remain the behavioral
specifications for their areas; this spec supplies the integration order, cross-cutting acceptance
requirements, and completion criteria.
